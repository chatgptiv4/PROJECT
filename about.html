About the institution

Federal University of Agriculture and Technology

FUNATO is presented here as a modern learning community where agriculture, computing, innovation, and national development meet.

Our vision

To nurture practical graduates who can solve real-world problems with technology, research, creativity, and responsible leadership.

The Computer Science Department supports this vision by teaching learners how to build useful software, manage data, understand networks, and solve problems with computing.

Core values

  • Academic excellence
  • Innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Integrity and public service
  • Technology for sustainable agriculture

Student learning path

01

Foundation

Understand computer systems, networks, web design, and digital citizenship.

Practice

Apply HTML, CSS, JavaScript, databases, and security tools in guided projects.

Build solutions that improve safety, productivity, and trust in digital services.
